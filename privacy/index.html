Privacy Policy — Clipper (Ascendant Wire)

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Clipper is a private tool used by Ascendant Wire to prepare and publish video clips to Ascendant Wire's own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X. It is not offered to the public.

What Clipper accesses. When the account owner signs in, each platform gives Clipper a sign-in token that lets it upload videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and schedule posts on that owner's own accounts. Clipper does not read, collect or store anyone else's personal information, messages, contacts or viewer data.

Where data is kept. Sign-in tokens and videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to anyone other than the platform being posted to.

YouTube. Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

TikTok. Clipper uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. It receives only the basic profile information TikTok provides (display name and account ID) and permission to upload videos to that account's inbox or post them. It does not access followers, messages, comments or any other TikTok data. Access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.

Facebook, Instagram and X. Clipper uses each platform's official API only to publish and schedule posts on the owner's own Page and accounts. It does not access followers, messages, comments or other people's data.

Removing access. Disconnecting an account in Clipper deletes its token from the computer. Access can also be revoked in each platform's own security settings.
